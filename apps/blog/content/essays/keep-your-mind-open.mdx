News has announced that SpaceX has acquired Cursor at 60B valuation ([new source], maybe twitter). I already knew it would be the case, and it was clear when the [60B/10B](source) collaboration was released to the public, when I discuss this case with my friend at XAI.

A few people on X has shared a story that they swiftly passed the opportunity to join Cursor, and has lost some once-in-a-lifetime oppourtnity. I also have a personal story to share:

one of the early member from the cursor team reached out to me via email in early 2024, when I was thinking about leaving Citadel. I was not sure what to do, but when I got the email, a rare one that I have replied among a lot of emails I received, I genuinely said that this sounds like an interesting product. However, the important part is I quickly rejected myself for them by telling them my tech stack doesn't match.

![screenshot of the email]()


In fact, I didn't even talk to them and ask about their tech stack. I was pretty good at Python, but had no experience with frontend, such as typescript or React. Even with python, i realize my time at Citadel has spent too much on the in-house tech stack but didn't have enough breadth of the knowledge about what is happening in the industry. I think one of the reason that I have rejected myself was because I did another startup quick call not so long ago before this email and that cofounder asked for Rust experience and FastAPI in python, which I was not familiar with. To be honest, it is quite stupid to feel rejected because I was not familiar with FastAPI because that stuff is super easy.

Anyway, the main point here is I should at least give it a try because this is one of the product that I actually would be interested in and with all the information later, they are a good team to work with. 

It is not the point that I could have joined them and became rich in an alternative universe. In fact, I probably would have failed the interview.

## Later story

About a bit more than a year later, Cursor reached out to me again, and I was in my previous startup. Due to a lot of reasons, I didn't really start a conversation with them for about another year in 2026, and I actually didn't pass their system design interview anyway. Partly because I was not prepared for the system design interview, partly because I was not that excited about the current situation of the product and the company. No matter what, I think it is clearly that the path won't cross.

The early member who reached out to me first has left Cursor around early 2024 as well. So I guess he also didn't ride the wave to the end.

## A lesson learned

Recently I always remind me of this lesson, to ask me to keep my mind open and talk to different people and different teams. There are lots of interesting teams out there doing interesting work. I have different levels of convictions about them, but I enjoyed all our conversations. I was also surprised that some founders are willing to grant a 1.5M / per equity to me as a founding engineer. Although the money is only paper money, it does show that there are founders who are very generous, and different founding engineers can get very different treatments and offers.
