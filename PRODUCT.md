# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering and product peers, potential collaborators, and thoughtful readers who want to understand Feitong Yang's work, judgment, and long-form thinking. They arrive with professional context, skim for signal, then decide whether to read deeper, follow the work, or reach out.

## Product Purpose

This is a personal blog and editorial home for essays, series, reading digests, and resources about technology, AI, product design, career, and human experience with digital tools. Success means the site feels like a durable intellectual workspace: easy to navigate, calm to read, and specific enough to convey taste and depth without presenting itself as a commercial product.

## Brand Personality

Clear, reflective, technically literate, and quietly distinctive. The voice should feel like an experienced practitioner thinking in public, not a company selling a platform.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site look like SaaS, a product landing page, a startup homepage, a conversion funnel, or a generic AI-generated portfolio. Avoid hero-metric templates, feature-card grids, glassy marketing panels, generic gradient spectacle, and copy that sounds like a value proposition.

## Design Principles

- Lead with writing and judgment, not promotion.
- Preserve editorial calm: the interface should make reading and browsing feel natural.
- Use interaction as atmosphere only when it does not obscure the content.
- Make navigation resilient and understandable before making it impressive.
- Let the design system support taste without turning every page into a component demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special compliance requirement has been set. Treat accessible semantics, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and robust no-JavaScript or slow-JavaScript fallbacks as a pragmatic quality baseline for a public personal site.
