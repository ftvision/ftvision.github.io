# Systematize the blog writing workflow

## Goal

Create a repeatable editorial workflow for turning raw ideas into published blog
essays and distribution assets, without flattening the author's voice or turning
the process into generic content marketing.

## Background

The blog is moving from a loose archive of essays, digests, and resource lists
toward a clearer personal IP around AI agents, product judgment, software work,
and decision-making in the agent era.

The writing process should help with four hard parts:

- finding a thesis with real friction
- choosing the right angle
- repairing structure before polishing prose
- converting each essay into distribution-native posts

## Proposed Workflow

```text
Capture -> Thesis -> Angle -> Outline -> Draft -> Structural Edit -> Line Edit -> Package -> Distribute -> Learn
```

## Acceptance Criteria

- A writer can take one messy idea and know the next step.
- The workflow separates thesis, structure, line editing, packaging, and distribution.
- The workflow explicitly preserves first-person builder voice.
- The workflow supports both English and Chinese publishing.
- The workflow produces at least one canonical blog essay and several platform-native distribution assets per flagship idea.

## Notes

Useful default collaboration points with Codex:

- Thesis: attack and sharpen the central claim.
- Angle: choose the most credible version of the piece.
- Outline: make every section do a job.
- Structural Edit: fix the essay's spine.
- Distribution: produce LinkedIn, X, newsletter, and optional Chinese variants.
