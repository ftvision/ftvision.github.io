## 1. Job-search blog revival
**Status:** outlining
**Form:** two-piece arc (Part 1 + Part 2), possibly more later

A pair of forward-looking wishlists separated by ~9 years. Reader gets the retrospective implicitly through contrast — no "look how naive I was" commentary needed.

**Part 1 — 2017 wishlist (existing draft, ship with light edits)**
- Source: `essays/job-search-reflection.mdx` (currently `draft: true`)
- Angle: end-of-PhD, what I wanted in a first industry role
- Editing pass: trim/restructure the list-heavy sections; keep the strong bits (collaborative-vs-competitive framing, "shit" feedback anecdote, academia→industry motivation); drop or compress "Expertise" and "Ranking" sections which read as self-report card
- Frame it as a time capsule — lean into the 2017-ness rather than modernizing the voice

**Part 2 — 2026 wishlist (new essay)**
- Angle: what I want in the next job I take
- Mirror Part 1's structure where it earns it; diverge where the categories no longer fit
- The interesting question: how have my criteria changed? (Don't write that essay — let the contrast do it.)

**Other related published pieces (not part of this arc, but adjacent):**
- `essays/offer-negotiation.mdx`
- `essays/reverse-interview-zh.mdx`
- `series/system-design-interview.mdx`

**Open questions:**
- Should Part 1 and Part 2 be linked (companion posts) or just live as standalone essays in the same topic?
- Title pattern? (e.g. "Job Search Reflection: 2017" / "Job Search Reflection: 2026")
